Don't Starve Together
--------------------
Don't Starve Together, its original item artwork, animations, sounds, character
and item designs, and other game assets belong to Klei Entertainment and their
respective rights holders. This project is a community mod, not an official
Klei product. A separately obtained copy of the game is required to play it.

The mod uses native game prefabs, animation banks and sounds at runtime.
Weapon engraving icons and some item cutouts are derived from native game
artwork, including assets_src/art_v1/originals/edge_*.png and corresponding
compiled icons/atlases under mod/ and site/dist/assets/. Mixed compiled atlases
may include both original project artwork and native game-derived images.
Do not treat those third-party images or mixed atlases as MIT-licensed artwork.
