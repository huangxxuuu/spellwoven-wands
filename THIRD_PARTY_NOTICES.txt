Don't Starve Together
--------------------
Don't Starve Together, its original item artwork, animations, sounds, character
and item designs, and other game assets belong to Klei Entertainment and their
respective rights holders. This project is a community mod, not an official
Klei product. A separately obtained copy of the game is required to play it.

artwork, including assets_src/art_v1/originals/edge_*.png and corresponding
compiled icons/atlases under mod/ and site/dist/assets/. Mixed compiled atlases

Instrument audio
----------------
The creator-supplied DST_Instrument_WAVs.zip contains rendered FreePats Ocarina
and Cithara Barbarica (medieval lyre) samples, supplied under CC0 1.0.
The original notices and CC0 texts are preserved in
assets_src/audio/instruments/licenses/ and mod/sound/licenses/.
Source/derivation manifests are retained in assets_src/audio/instruments/.
The 76 WAVs are packed without resampling, pitch randomization or PCM changes
into mod/sound/nw_instruments.fsb; nw_instruments.fev maps individual pitches.
These audio assets retain their supplied CC0 terms, independently of the
project's code license. The FMOD compiler/runtime is not redistributed.
