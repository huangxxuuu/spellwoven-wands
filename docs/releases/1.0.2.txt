织法之杖 1.0.2 · 施法方向设置
2026-11-08

1. 新增「施法方向」设置：可在游戏内打开「向导」随时切换，默认使用「目标选择」。设置自动保存在本机，仅对当前玩家生效，不影响世界设置或其他玩家。

2. 目标选择：选中物体时，朝它的实际位置施法。例如点击 Boss 的头部，也会朝它脚下的实际位置瞄准。

3. 鼠标方向：始终朝鼠标对应的地面位置施法，不受鼠标悬停物体影响。适合需要精确瞄准地面位置的情况。

Spellwoven Wands 1.0.2 · Cast Direction

1. Added Cast Direction: open the in-game Guide to switch at any time. Target Selection is the default. Your choice is saved locally and affects only your player, without changing world settings or other players.

2. Target Selection: when an object is selected, cast toward its actual position. For example, selecting a boss's head aims toward its position on the ground.

3. Mouse Direction: always cast toward the ground position under the cursor, regardless of any hovered object. Useful for aiming at a precise spot on the ground.
