[h1]Spellwoven Wands: Weave Your Next Spell[/h1]
[b]Version 1.0.0 is out now[/b]
[url=https://huangxxuuu.github.io/spellwoven-wands/en/]English field guide & full spell catalog[/url] | [url=https://github.com/huangxxuuu/spellwoven-wands/issues]Report an issue[/url]

Put Homing before a Spark Bolt and watch it turn toward an enemy. Load a spinning axe into an impact payload and let an arrow deliver your melee attack from afar. After the fight, swap a few cards to harvest, fish, or play a melody at camp.

Inspired by Noita, Spellwoven Wands brings wand building to Don't Starve Together. [b]Collect wands and spell cards, arrange their order, and create your own attacks, tools, and musical phrases.[/b]

[h2]1. Different Cards, Different Spells[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Homing → Spark Bolt[/b]: make a straight-flying spark turn toward its target.
[*][b]Double Spell → Spark Bolt → Magic Arrow[/b]: release two different spells in one cast.
[*][b]Trident Cast[/b]: send a group of spells in different directions. Then experiment with piercing, bouncing, or orbiting.
[/list]
Slots, maximum mana, regeneration, cast delay, and recharge all affect how a wand feels. [b]Non-shuffle wands follow card order; shuffle wands randomize it.[/b] A good build needs a wand that suits it.

[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/11171490399105963216/E590C6967A155BB63DFEC178656974985A049A34/[/img]

[h2]2. Save the Next Spell for an Impact or a Timer[/h2]
[b]Magic Arrow · Collision → Spinning Axe Attack[/b] releases a spinning axe where the arrow hits. Switch to a timed payload to change when it activates. The same payload can play differently with a different trigger.
Explosion is an on-hit modifier: put it before a projectile to make its impacts explode. You can also prepare Proximity Crystal Mines, Dormant Crystals, and Hollow Payload Eggs to leave spells waiting in the world.

[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/15118734145080234800/DD32D4BC40C47FC2C7425A9B5ADD4E1D9381E50B/[/img]

[h2]3. Turn Familiar Weapons into Spells[/h2]
At the Weapon Engraving Table, combine blank cards with the weapons, tools, and materials listed in a recipe. Spears, axes, and Glass Cutters gain a new way to strike.
A [b]Shadow Reaper[/b] can harvest grass and saplings along its path, collecting nearby yields into your inventory when possible. Swap your cards, and your fighting wand becomes a helping hand.

[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/14707989744641070350/A874713288E0285ADC1C71C6271CAAEF89DCC094/[/img]

[h2]4. Take Your Wand Ocean Fishing[/h2]
Throw [b]Chum[/b] into the sea to attract fish, then cast an [b]Ocean Fishing Rod[/b] spell. Its hook brings caught ocean fish or floating objects toward a nearby shore or boat in the caster's direction.
Other tools include a Freshwater Fishing Rod for ponds and a Garden Hoe for farmland. Pack the right cards for your destination.

[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/9826035569134835751/BFCB0B4E96A0E274E2B64262B96531CC7D941F6C/[/img]

[h2]5. Take the Starlight with You[/h2]
Star Caller's Staff and Moon Caller's Staff spells create vanilla Dwarf Stars and Polar Lights. Movement, homing, and target-selection cards can make them follow the player.
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/15595512404166230674/D5F63D9EFFA3B91957BD16992B15AF9F7A7EF759/[/img]

Add [b]Giant Shot[/b] to enlarge a star and the reach of its light, temperature, and sanity effects. It also enlarges applicable spells and their effective reach, [b]without directly increasing damage[/b]. Star spells retain their dedicated minimum cooldowns.

[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/10479249389854033689/F11DA360F9753BEAB4B950611E0A6176709655C3/[/img]

[h2]6. Collect, Arrange, Rearrange[/h2]
[olist]
[*][b]Pick up your Starter Wand.[/b] By default, your first arrival in a world grants a two-slot wand loaded with Spark Bolt. Equip it and right-click to cast. Hold right-click while standing still to keep casting.
[*][b]Build an Arcane Workbench.[/b] Near an Alchemy Engine, search for the station by name. It costs 2 Boards, 2 Cut Stone, 4 Gold Nuggets, and 2 Purple Gems.
[*][b]Start changing cards.[/b] Equip your wand near the workbench, then click or drag cards to edit it. Try Damage Plus → Spark Bolt before adding more cards.
[/olist]
Each station has a different job:
[list]
[*][b]Arcane Workbench[/b]: grind gems, craft wands and cards, and edit your wand.
[*][b]Weapon Engraving Table[/b]: turn recipe weapons and tools into spell cards.
[*][b]Arcane Recycler[/b]: recover Arcane Dust from unwanted cards, piles, and wands lying nearby. Put anything you want to keep safely away first.
[/list]
[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/9463565764347824291/6C4F2133ED5C287970A12E01F87D30D2A6C091D8/[/img]
[i]Left to right: Arcane Workbench, Weapon Engraving Table, Arcane Recycler.[/i]

Collect cards through crafting, engraving, creature drops, and boss rewards. Some are loot-only. Wands have different rarities and randomized stats. Stack loose cards into piles of up to [b]25[/b], and name your wands with a Feather Pencil.
To compare damage, craft a [b]Spell Test Punching Bag[/b] at a Science Machine with 3 Cut Grass and 1 Board. Consecutive hits add to its displayed damage total.

[img]https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/17915763130818327811/845B364A802483D4788F7642CE2477FF45928F4C/[/img]

[h2]7. Your Wand Can Be an Instrument[/h2]
Arrange [b]ocarina or kantele notes[/b] in your wand and play a musical phrase for camp. Both instruments have base notes covering C, D, E, F, G, A, and B. Semitone and octave modifiers adjust pitch.
Speed Up and Speed Down select half-beat and two-beat note samples. [b]They do not automatically synchronize repeated casts to a musical tempo.[/b] Timing still depends on wand stats, casting animations, and manual wand changes.
The gameplay demonstration uses kantele cards to play a short excerpt of [b]Deep Sea Girl (Shinkai Shoujo)[/b]. The card order forms the melody; the casts produce the in-game sound.
